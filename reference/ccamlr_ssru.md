# CCAMLR Small Scale Research Units

CCAMLR Small Scale Research Units (SSRUs).

## Usage

``` r
ccamlr_ssru
```

## Format

Simple feature collection with 19 features and 13 fields:

- GAR_ID:

  CCAMLR Statistical Areas

- Name:

  CCAMLR Statistical Areas

- ShortLabel:

  CCAMLR Statistical Areas

- LongLabel:

  CCAMLR Statistical Areas

## Source

CCAMLR <https://spatial.ccamlr.org/>
